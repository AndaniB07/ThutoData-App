ABOUT THUTODATA

Helping students find their route to university.

ThutoData is a student project designed to make university and funding information easier to find, understand and use.

About ThutoData

ThutoData is a student project created for the INL 370 Capstone module at the University of Pretoria.

The platform provides students with simplified information about universities, colleges, bursaries, applications and other opportunities for further education.

OUR MISSION

Making information easier to understand.

To remove information barriers for rural students in Limpopo and KwaZulu-Natal by providing simplified university admissions and bursaries information.

We also aim to build digital literacy skills that will serve students throughout their academic careers and beyond.

WHO ARE WE?

WHY WE BUILT THIS

Information should not be a barrier to opportunity.

Many students in rural communities face challenges when trying to access clear and reliable information about university applications and funding.

Finding information is only one part of the challenge. Students also need the digital literacy skills required to navigate online application systems and make informed decisions about their education.

ThutoData is our attempt to help bridge that gap by bringing important information together in one accessible platform.

Our goal

Give students the information and digital skills they need to make confident decisions about their future.
