Application Process

Your Step-by-Step Guide to Applying for Higher Education

Follow these simple steps to apply for university, college, and TVET courses with confidence.

1
Check Your Subject Match

What to do

Use our Subject-to-Course Matching Tool to enter your Grade 11 or Grade 12 subjects and latest marks.

Why it matters

Instantly find courses and qualifying programs that match your exact subject combination and Admission Point Score (APS).

💡 Tip: Keep your June or final Grade 11 report nearby so you can enter your exact percentages.
2
Gather Your Required Documents

Before starting any online application, get certified digital copies or clear photos of:

  • South African ID or Birth Certificate (Certified Copy)
  • Grade 11 Final Report or Grade 12 June/Trial Results (Certified Copy)
  • Parent/Guardian's ID copy certified and Proof of Income (needed for funding and bursaries)
💡 Pro Tip: Store these documents in a folder on your phone so they are ready to upload anytime!
3
Choose Your Institutions & Programs
  • Select up to 2–3 study options per institution — a first choice and a backup option.
  • Double-check specific faculty requirements, such as minimum Mathematics or English pass rates.
4
Submit Your Applications

University / College Direct Portals

Follow the step-by-step application links provided in our directory.

Central Applications Office (CAO)

If applying to institutions in KwaZulu-Natal, submit through the CAO portal using one application form.

5
Apply for Funding & Bursaries

Don't wait for acceptance letters to start applying for funding!

  • Check our Bursary Directory for NSFAS and non-NSFAS corporate, government, and private bursaries.
  • Complete funding applications before their respective closing dates.
6
Track & Respond
  • Keep your application reference numbers and login details safe.
  • Log into institution student portals regularly to monitor your application status.
  • Accept firm or provisional offers promptly before the response deadlines.

You've Got This! 🎓

Take your time, check your information carefully, and keep track of every application you submit.
