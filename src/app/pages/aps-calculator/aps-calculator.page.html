Important Information

  • Your APS is calculated using your six recognised subjects.
  • Life Orientation is not included in the APS total.
  • The maximum APS is 42.
  • Different universities and programmes may have different APS requirements.
  • Meeting the minimum APS does not guarantee admission. Universities may also consider specific subject requirements, available spaces and other selection criteria.
